# 🐄 VIDEO 1 — गाई बुढीको गीत (The Cow's Song)

**Video Prompt:** [High-quality cinematic 2D Nepali cartoon animation, clean bold outlines, soft hand-painted village backgrounds, warm natural color palette, expressive oversized eyes, smooth frame-by-frame motion, fast beat-synced cuts, dynamic speed-ramping, ultra-clean line art, vibrant saturated colors, detailed Nepali hill-village setting (stone-and-mud houses, tin roofs, terraced fields, chautari peepal tree), consistent character models, film-grade composition, TikTok/Reels-paced editing.]  A glossy brown-and-white village cow stands at the center of a golden courtyard, marigold garlands strung overhead, milk-splash sparkle hearts drifting around her. She bounces happily on each beat, tail flicking, udder gently swaying, a brass milk pot glinting beside her. Camera: slow orbit building into a fast push-zoom on the final beat, ending on a freeze of her joyful moo.
**On-screen Text (beat-timed):** 🐄 **"गाई बुढी"** → **"दूधकी रानी"** → **"गाउँको भरोसा"**

**🎵 THEME SONG — "गाई बुढीको गीत" (10 sec, 4 lines, one per ~2.5s):**
> गाई बुढी, गाई बुढी, दूधै दूधकी रानी हो,
> सेतो दूधमा शक्ति भरिने, गाउँकी जीवनदायिनी हो,
> बिहान बेलुका दूध दिन्छे, कहिल्यै नगरी आनाकानी,
> गाई बुढी हाम्रो माया, गाई बुढी हाम्रो कहानी!

**Voice Direction:** Children's chorus, bouncy sing-song, "रानी" and "कहानी" stretched playfully for rhyme-payoff.
**SFX:** Cow moo synced on beat 1, milk-splash sparkle chime, hand claps under the chorus.
**Music:** Madal + flute loop, 148 BPM, one whistle-hook drop on the last line.
**Ending Hook:** Freeze on the cow's proud pose, stamp: ❤️ **"गाई बुढी — दूधकी रानी"** → 🔻 पूरा कथा YouTube मा ▶

**Why 5 separate songs works better than 1 combined video:**
- Each character gets a **full emotional beat + their own hook**, not a rushed 2-second cameo — stronger parasocial attachment per character.
- **5 posts instead of 1** = 5x the algorithm shots on the discovery page across the week.
- Every video ends on the same CTA, so all five funnel independently into the full story — viewers who only ever see "बिमलको गीत" still get pulled to YouTube.
- Character-specific hashtags let each clip rank for a different search/interest cluster (कोमल mothers relate to Sita, kids relate to Bimal, etc.).
