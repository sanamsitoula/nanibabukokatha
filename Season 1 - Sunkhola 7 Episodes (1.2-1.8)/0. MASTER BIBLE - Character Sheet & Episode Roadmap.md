**[GOPAL]** — 35-year-old Nepali village milkman. Medium-brown skin, short black side-parted hair under a **black-and-white dhaka topi**, thick black eyebrows, gentle tired brown eyes, light stubble, lean sturdy body, 5'7". Wears a **faded grey-green half-sleeve shirt**, dark brown trousers rolled to the ankles, worn rubber sandals, and a **red-checked gamchha (scarf) over his left shoulder**. Always carries a **brass milk theki (container)**. Voice: warm, low, slightly weary male voice.
*Season-1 arc:* After confessing in 1.1, Gopal is now the village's respected moral anchor — he delivers free pure milk to the school and mentors others through honesty.

**[BIMAL]** — 8-year-old boy, Gopal's son. Round face, light-brown skin, big curious brown eyes, messy black hair with a small cowlick, small thin frame, slightly pale cheeks. Wears a **light-blue school shirt, navy shorts**, worn black sandals, small cloth shoulder bag. Voice: soft, sweet, energetic child voice.
*Season-1 arc:* Bimal has recovered his strength after the pure-milk resolution; across these 7 episodes he grows from a tempted child into a morally courageous boy.

**[SITA]** — 32-year-old village mother. Fair-brown skin, hair in a neat low bun with a red ribbon, small gold nose stud, kind oval face. Wears a **maroon-and-green printed cotton gunyu-cholo (sari-style)**, red glass bangles, green pote necklace. Voice: gentle, caring female voice.
*Season-1 arc:* The quiet heart of the family; episode 1.5 centres on her silent sacrifice.

**[KRISHNA]** — 36-year-old milk middleman, Gopal's childhood friend. Slightly stocky, wheatish skin, **oiled black hair combed straight back, thin black mustache**, narrow sly eyes. ## 🗺️ 7-EPISODE ROADMAP (full plot outlines)

### EPISODE 1.2 — Honesty & Truth
**Title:** झूटको जिब्रो, रोएको मन *(Lying tongue, weeping heart)*
**Moral:** One lie breeds a hundred; only truth sets the heart free.
**Plot:** At the Saturday haat bazaar, Bimal finds a folded cloth pouch dropped near the chautari — inside, a thin gold earring + a 500-rupee note, belonging to old Budhi Aamaa (her only savings for cataract eye surgery at the city eye camp). Tempted (family still poor post-debt), Bimal pockets it. At home he lies to Sita ("a friend gifted it"). The lie snowballs: he buys biscuits, buys popularity at school, becomes "rich Bimal" for a day — but each step his stomach knots. Meanwhile Budhi Aamaa is seen weeping, searching the chautari, her surgery slipping away. Nirmala Miss announces the loss at school assembly. Bimal's guilt peaks. Gopal, sensing something, gently tells Bimal his OWN story (callback to 1.1) — "ढुङ्गा लुकाउन खोज्दा थप्किन्छ, सत्य बोल्दा पग्लिन्छ।" Climax: Bimal walks to the chautari in front of the gathered village, returns the pouch, and apologises. Budhi Aamaa blesses him with tears. Resolution: Bimal sleeps peacefully for the first time in days — truth as relief. Final moral card.
**Ending callback:** Gopal's recovered theki of pure milk sits beside Bimal — the parallel of "pure" (शुद्ध) carried from father to son.

---

### EPISODE 1.3 — Forgiveness & Second Chances
**Title:** पापको भारी, क्षमाको फूल *(Burden of sin, flower of forgiveness)*
**Moral:** True forgiveness costs the forgiver — but a grudge costs far more.
**Plot:** Krishna has faithfully delivered free pure milk to the school for months. He is changing — quieter, kinder, mends his old bicycle instead of flaunting. But a jealous rival milkman (Sahuji's cousin) spreads a FALSE rumour: "कृष्णले फेरि पानी हाल्यो!" The village turns cold; some refuse to speak to Krishna; Nirmala Miss is pressured to stop his milk. Krishna, broken ("म बदलिएँ नि... कोही विश्वास गर्दैन"), packs to leave Sunkhola at dawn. The milk bottle is secretly tested again by Hari Doctor — it's purest yet. But the result comes too late: Krishna is already gone. Gopal — whom Krishna betrayed most deeply in 1.1 — rides after him on the dirt road, stops him, and in front of the waking village declares "मैले माफ गरें, गाउँले पनि माफ गरून्।" The rumour-monger is exposed. Resolution: Krishna returns, weeps, is welcomed back. Final moral card on forgiveness.

---

### EPISODE 1.4 — Hard Work vs Shortcuts
**Title:** छिटोको बाटो, लामो पछुतो *(The fast road, the long regret)*
**Moral:** There is no shortcut to anything worth growing.
**Plot:** The school announces a vegetable-garden competition — grow your own, judged at harvest. Bimal works his small plot daily (watering, weeding) with Gopal. Enter **Birsedai**, the slick peddler, selling "जादुई हुर्काउने खाद्य" (miracle growth powder — actually just coloured sugar) to village farmers, promising double harvest in half the time. Gopal, still recovering the debt, is tempted to buy some for his own terraced field. Bimal, watching his honest slow sprouts, almost doubts himself. Twist: Bimal overhears Birsedai laughing about the fraud at the tea shop and realises. He races home to stop Gopal — but Gopal, proud father moment, has already refused: "छोरा, छिटो उम्रिएको बिरुवा पनि छिटो मर्छ।" Climax: harvest day — those who used the powder have wilted yellow plants; Bimal's honest plot yields strong healthy vegetables, wins the prize. Birsedai exposed, chased out. Final moral card.

---

### EPISODE 1.5 — Family & Sacrifice (Sita focus)
**Title:** आमाको खाली थाली, छोराको मुस्कान *(Mother's empty plate, child's smile)*
**Moral:** The deepest love is the one that feeds you and goes hungry itself.
**Plot:** A big district-level school race approaches — the winner gets a scholarship track-kit. Bimal desperately wants proper running shoes (his sandals are torn). The family cannot afford them. Sita, silently, walks two hills to the pawn-broker and pawns her last gold nose-stud (her wedding gift) to buy Bimal real shoes. She hides it, eats smaller and smaller portions, claims "म त खाँदै खाँदै थकित" — Bimal sees her empty plate, her thinning wrists. Twist: Sita faints in the goth one morning. Hari Doctor diagnoses exhaustion + hunger, not disease. Bimal, searching her trunk for a blanket, finds the pawn ticket and the truth hits him. Climax: Bimal refuses to wear the shoes to the race, runs barefoot — but first returns to the pawn-broker with Gopal and the village women's pooled money to redeem the nose-stud. Sita wears it again as Bimal wins. Final moral card.

---

### EPISODE 1.6 — Friendship & Loyalty
**Title:** साथीको साइनो, ढुङ्गाको माइनो *(Bond of a friend, the rock in the mine)*
**Moral:** A real friend stands by you when the whole world walks away.
**Plot:** Introduce **Suman**, Bimal's best friend, the poorest barefoot boy in class. Sahuji's shop is robbed one night (a tin box of small cash). Next morning Suman is accused — he was seen near the shop at dusk (he was only returning a dropped vegetable sack). The whole school turns on Suman; even other kids' parents tell them to stay away. Bimal is pressured to drop Suman too. Bimal, loyal, refuses, and quietly investigates with Suman: they retrace steps, find a muddy footprint too big for a child, a torn piece of patterned cloth caught on the shop window. Twist: the print matches Birsedai's loud checked shirt (he passed through) — OR a richer boy's father. Resolution: Bimal presents the evidence at the chautari; Suman cleared; the real thief (Birsedai, caught passing through again) exposed. Suman weeps with gratitude. Final moral card on loyalty.

---

### EPISODE 1.7 — Greed & its Consequences
**Title:** लोभको खाडल, इमानको पुल *(The chasm of greed, the bridge of integrity)*
**Moral:** Greed digs its own grave; integrity is the bridge that lasts.
**Plot:** **Bansa Bahadur**, the rich moneylender-landgrabber, arrives in Sunkhola offering "easy loans, no interest for 3 months" to trap struggling farmers in debt-for-land swaps. Sahuji, greedy, becomes his local agent, persuading villagers to sign thumbprint contracts they can't read. Gopal is offered a bribe (cash) to stay silent and even to convince others — he refuses flat. Several families sign away. Krishna (now reformed) recognises the trap from his own past greed and warns the village. Twist: when the 3 months end, Bansa Bahadur demands land; the contracts were rigged. Panic. Climax: Gopal + Krishna + Hari Doctor + Nirmala Miss organise the village; they bring the district officer / police; Bansa Bahadur's fraud is exposed, contracts voided. Sahuji, shamed, repents and tears up his agent commission. The greedy lose; Gopal's integrity is honoured by the village. Final moral card.

---

### EPISODE 1.8 — Education & Dreams (Season Finale)
**Title:** बल्ने दीप, सपनाको आँखा *(The burning lamp, the eye of dreams)*
**Moral:** Education is the lamp that lights not just one life, but a whole village.
**Plot:** Bimal, inspired by Hari Doctor and Nirmala Miss, dreams of becoming the village's first doctor/teacher. A prestigious district scholarship exam (full boarding school) is announced in the city — but the bus fare + exam form cost money the family doesn't have, and doubters mock: "किसानको छोरो किसान नै।" Bimal studies every night by the dim tuki lamp (the recurring "burning lamp"), falling asleep on his books. Conflict: Gopal sells his prized second cow to fund the trip (callback — the cows were nearly lost in 1.1); Sita gives her redeemed nose-stud money; reformed Krishna lends his bicycle for the long road; Nirmala Miss coaches him extra hours; Suman cheers him on. Twist: at the exam, Bimal is overwhelmed by city kids with tutors — a moment of near-defeat — but he remembers the village lamp, his father's pure-milk oath, and writes from the heart. Resolution: whether he wins or not, the episode ends on hope — the letter arrives, Bimal wins the scholarship. The whole village sees him off at the chautari, Gopal's eyes wet but proud. Final scene: the tuki lamp burns in Bimal's old study corner — empty seat, but the light stays. Moral card. **End-of-season tag:** "अर्को सिजनमा..." tease.
