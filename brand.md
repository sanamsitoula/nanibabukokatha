Master brand reference for launching the channel. Copy-paste the prompt blocks
directly into your AI image/video tool (Midjourney, Ideogram, Leonardo, Sora,
Kling, CapCut AI, etc.) — each one is written as a ready-to-use prompt, not a
description.

## 1. Brand Name

**Primary (recommended):** नानीबाबुको कथा — *Nani Babu Ko Katha*
("The Little Ones' Stories" / "Children's Tales")

Why this wins: it's already your working project name, it's warm and
grandmother-voice in tone, it's easy to say for Nepali parents searching
YouTube, and it romanizes cleanly for the English title field
(`Nani Babu Ko Katha`) so both Devanagari and Latin search traffic land on
the same channel.

**Backup options** (only if नानीबाबुको कथा is taken as a handle):
- सुनखोला कथा घर (Sunkhola Katha Ghar) — ties to the village setting used in your scripts
- चौतारी कथा (Chautari Katha) — "story at the village rest-stop," strong visual anchor
- हाम्रो नानी कथा (Hamro Nani Katha)

**Handle strategy:** Lock the identical handle across all platforms before
anything else:
`@nanibabukokatha` on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram. If unavailable anywhere,
fall back to `@nanibabukokatha.official` everywhere (consistency > cleverness).

## 8. Thumbnail Prompts (per story)

Thumbnails are **static images**, separate from the intro bumper (Section 6,
first 3 seconds of the *video*) and the outro card (Section 7, last 5
seconds of the *video*). Never reuse a bumper frame as the thumbnail — the
thumbnail's only job is stopping the scroll/search-scan; it needs to read
instantly at 168×94px (mobile search size), which the intro/outro
compositions are not designed for.

Generate at 1280×720 (YouTube's required minimum), export a 9:16 crop for
Shorts/TikTok/Reels cover frames from the same source image.

### 8a. Main Thumbnail Prompt (use for every story — swap the bracketed fields)

```
Cinematic 2D Nepali cartoon animation thumbnail, 1280x720, high contrast,
mobile-readable at small size. Extreme close-up of [CHARACTER NAME]'s face
showing [SHOCKED / TEARFUL / DEFIANT — pick the story's strongest emotion],
large expressive eyes, exact character reference: [paste character
description from that story's character sheet]. Background: blurred/
darkened version of [KEY LOCATION, e.g. the village chautari / the goth at
night], warm terracotta-to-maroon gradient vignette pulling focus to the
face. Bold Devanagari hook text overlay, 3-5 words max, thick white text
with maroon outline and soft drop shadow, positioned in the upper or lower
third (not covering the face): "[HOOK TEXT — the single most shocking line
from the story, e.g. आफ्नै छोरालाई धोका?!]". Small circular
नानीबाबुको कथा logo badge in a bottom corner. No clutter, no more than one
secondary object/prop in frame, thumbnail must read clearly even at 120px
wide.
```

### 8b. Alternate "Mystery/Question" Thumbnail Prompt (A/B test option)

```
Cinematic 2D Nepali cartoon animation thumbnail, 1280x720. Two-character
split composition: [CHARACTER A] on the left looking [EMOTION], [CHARACTER
B] on the right looking [OPPOSITE/CONTRASTING EMOTION], exact character
references: [paste both character descriptions]. A bold red or golden
arrow, circle, or glow highlights the key story object (e.g. a red
jerrycan / a torn letter / a hidden coin) between them. Warm terracotta
background with darkened edges (vignette). Large bold Devanagari question
text at top: "[QUESTION HOOK, e.g. दूध कि पानी?]". Small circular
नानीबाबुको कथा logo badge bottom corner. High contrast, no clutter.
```

**Formula for picking hook text:** pull directly from that story's
strongest "Scene Ending Hook" line in the script (your scripts already
write these) — do not write new copy, the twist lines are already
thumbnail-ready.

**A/B testing note:** YouTube Studio lets you test up to 3 thumbnails per
video — generate one from 8a (character-emotion) and one from 8b
(mystery-object) per story and let the platform pick the winner after
~1,000 impressions.

## 11. Platform Playbook

### YouTube (main hub — long-form 2–4 min stories)
- **Cadence:** 1 full story per week minimum (you already have 50 story
  ideas banked — that's ~1 year of weekly uploads without writing a new
  premise).
- **Best posting time (Nepal audience, IST/NPT evenings):** Fri or Sat,
  6:00–8:00 PM NPT — catches after-school/after-work family screen time
  and weekend viewing.
- **Also post:** the same story cut into a **YouTube Short** (60s, the
  strongest single twist scene + moral line) 1–2 days *before* the full
  story drops, as a teaser that funnels into the full video via end-screen
  and pinned comment link.
- **Thumbnails:** terracotta/cream palette, one shocking character
  expression close-up + 3–5 word Devanagari hook text, no more.

### TikTok
- **Format:** Repurpose the same Shorts cut (≤60s, ideally 21–34s for
  completion rate). Use the strongest cliffhanger scene, not the whole
  story — TikTok rewards loopable/complete-feeling clips.
- **Cadence:** 3–4x per week. Mix: (a) story-twist clips from
  already-published YouTube stories, (b) character "did you know" or
  moral-of-the-day text-over-clip posts, (c) behind-the-scenes/character
  art reveals to build parasocial interest before a new story drops.
- **Best posting time:** 7:00–9:00 PM NPT and a second slot 12:00–1:00 PM
  NPT (lunch scroll).
- **Caption formula:** one-line hook + moral hashtag, e.g. "दूधमा पानी
  मिसाउनुको यस्तो नतिजा हुन्छ थाहा थिएन 😢 #नेपालीकथा #moralstory"
- **Sound:** use trending Nepali/Hindi folk-adjacent audio when it fits a
  village-life clip; otherwise keep your own baked-in dialogue audio (it's
  a strength — real narration outperforms generic trending sound for story
  content).

### Instagram Reels
- **Format:** same vertical cut as TikTok, re-exported (don't repost with
  TikTok watermark — always upload the clean source file to each platform
  natively for reach).
- **Cadence:** 3x per week, can mirror TikTok's calendar exactly.
- **Extras:** Instagram rewards carousel posts too — post a 4–6 slide
  carousel of your character art (`character/` folder assets) with one
  moral quote per slide once every week or two; use Stories daily for
  polls ("अब गोपालले के गर्ला?") to drive engagement before a story drops.
- **Bio link:** link-in-bio to the latest YouTube video, always.
- **Hashtags (mix broad + niche, 8–12):** #नेपालीकथा #NepaliStory
  #MoralStory #NepaliCartoon #NepaliAnimation #KidsStory #StoryTime
  #नैतिककथा #NepaliContent #Reels

### Cross-platform funnel logic
YouTube = the archive/monetization home. TikTok + Reels = discovery
engines that exist to push new viewers to YouTube. Every short-form post
should end with (verbally or on-screen) "पूरा कथा YouTube मा" (full story
on YouTube) and the channel handle.

## 14. Monetization Roadmap

YouTube's Partner Program alone is slow to reach (1,000 subs + 4,000 watch
hours, or 10M Shorts views/90 days) — don't wait on it exclusively. Stack
these in parallel from day one:

### Fastest-to-first-revenue (weeks, not months)
1. **TikTok Creator Rewards Program** — pays per 1,000+ views on videos
   ≥1 minute once you hit 10K followers + 100K views/30 days. Your
   30–60s story-twist clips qualify once cut slightly longer (aim for
   60–90s cuts specifically for TikTok, not just the 21–34s teaser).
2. **Instagram Reels bonuses / Meta creator monetization** (where
   available in Nepal/region) — similar per-view model, no subscriber
   threshold in some rollouts.
3. **Brand sponsorships from Nepali businesses** — kids' snack brands,
   ed-tech apps (Nepali learning apps), publishers, telecom/family data
   plans, toy/stationery brands. A channel with a clear moral-story niche
   and child+parent audience is an easy sell even at 5–10K subscribers —
   pitch directly once you have 10+ published stories as a portfolio.
4. **Nepali diaspora audience** — a meaningful share of your view/watch-
   time will come from Nepali families abroad (Gulf, Malaysia, India,
   US/UK/Australia). These are higher-CPM ad markets once YPP is active;
   worth calling out explicitly in early sponsorship pitches too.

### Medium-term (3–6 months)
5. **YouTube Partner Program (ads + Shorts fund)** — the baseline goal;
   your weekly-upload + Shorts-teaser cadence (Section 12) is specifically
   designed to hit both watch-hours and Shorts-views thresholds
   simultaneously, whichever qualifies first.
6. **Channel memberships / Super Thanks** (once YPP unlocked) — low
   effort once eligible, offer members early access to next week's story
   or a monthly bonus story.
7. **Patreon / Buy Me a Coffee** — doesn't require any platform threshold,
   can launch immediately. Works well for a channel with an emotional,
   loyal following (your comment sections will likely be full of parents
   — that's your Patreon audience).
8. **Digital products** — sell the story scripts as an illustrated Nepali
   moral-story eBook/PDF bundle (parents buy these for bedtime reading),
   or a printable coloring book using the `character/` art assets. Low
   production cost since the art already exists per story.

### Longer-term / higher-ceiling
9. **Licensing to Nepali TV/OTT platforms** (e.g. regional kids'
   programming blocks, Nepali streaming apps) once you have a full season
   (50 stories = a real catalog to license).
10. **Merchandise** — character plushies/stickers/school supplies
    featuring Gopal, Bimal, etc., sold via Instagram/TikTok shop once the
    characters have recognition.
11. **Crowdfunding a "Season 2" or spin-off** (Nepali crowdfunding
    platforms or GoFundMe within diaspora communities) once the channel
    has a track record — parents/diaspora often fund culturally-
    meaningful children's content directly.

### Priority order for month 1–3
Focus on #1–3 (TikTok Rewards + Instagram bonuses + first brand pitch)
since they don't gate on YouTube's subscriber/watch-hour thresholds, while
the weekly YouTube uploads accumulate watch hours toward YPP in the
background.
