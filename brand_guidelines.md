# नानीबाबुको कथा (Nani Babu Ko Katha) — Brand Guidelines

The short, everyday rulebook for the channel and the website (`website/`). The full production prompts
(logo, banner, intro/outro bumpers, thumbnails, SEO templates) live in `brand.md`; characters and the
Season 1 roadmap live in `Season 1 - Sunkhola 7 Episodes (1.2-1.8)/0. MASTER BIBLE…md`. This file does
not repeat them.

---

## 1. Brand

**नानीबाबुको कथा** — original Nepali animated moral stories for kids and families, set in the hill village
of **सुनखोला (Sunkhola)**: village life, children, animals and family, each ending with a lesson worth
remembering.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tagline (Nepali, primary) | **हरेक कथामा एउटा पाठ, हरेक पाठमा एउटा माया।** (Every story a lesson, every lesson a little love.) |
| Short tagline | **साना कथा, ठूला पाठ।** (Small stories, big lessons.) |
| English line | Nepali animated moral stories for kids and families. Small stories, big lessons. |
| Handle | `@nanibabukokatha` everywhere (fallback `@nanibabukokatha.official`) |

## 2. Audience

Nepali children (5–12) and the parents and grandparents watching with them, in Nepal and abroad; also
diaspora families who want children to hear Nepali and know village life.

## 3. Voice

Warm, grandmother-at-the-chautari storytelling. Nepali first, simple words, emotion before lesson.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Open on a hook or a question ("दूध कि पानी?") | Lecture; state the moral before the story earns it |
| Let characters fail, feel it, and choose better | Mock poverty, caste, region or disability |
| End with one clear moral card in Devanagari | Violence, fear for its own sake, or adult themes |
| Mix Nepali with light romanised English only in titles/SEO | Hinglish-style slang in the story itself |

## 4. Logo

Circular badge: a girl and boy (nani-babu) under a peepal tree on a stone chautari, one holding a glowing
storybook, hills and a tiled-roof house behind, "नानीबाबुको कथा" arched across the top in cream with maroon
outline, maroon rim. Source: intro bumper `common/0.0_Entry_Scene_*.mp4`; web copy: `website/assets/logo.png`.

- Use the full badge for avatars, intros, the website and thumbnails (small, bottom corner).
- Icon-only version (no text) for favicons and app icons (`brand.md` §3).
- Keep the badge on cream or warm backgrounds; never recolour, stretch, or crop the rim.

| Body | **Mukta** 400/600 | very readable Devanagari and Latin at small sizes |

## 7. Visual style

2D flat storybook cartoon, bold clean outlines, warm golden-hour light, pahadi village: terraced fields,
stone houses, tin and tiled roofs, the chautari peepal tree. Characters must match their master sheets
exactly (`1.1 dudh ma pani/character/*.png`).

## 9. Story formula (`brand.md` §9)

Intro bumper (3s) → hook → story (narration, dialogue, scene-ending cliffhangers) → moral card in
Devanagari, spoken aloud → outro with subscribe nudge (5–6s).
