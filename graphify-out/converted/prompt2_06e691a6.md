## PROMPT 2 — Write a Highly Addictive Nepali Animated Moral Story
[यहाँ Story Title राख्नुहोस्]
Write a highly addictive, emotional and cinematic Nepali animated moral story for YouTube.
The story must be written in natural, simple and conversational Nepali language with realistic Nepali dialogues. The story must be set entirely in Nepal, especially in an authentic Nepali village environment.
### Story Formula:
Hook → Mystery → Emotional Conflict → Betrayal → Big Twist → Struggle → Unexpected Climax → Powerful Moral
### Rules:
1. Powerful Hook
The very first line must immediately create curiosity, shock or emotion.
Do not begin with boring background exposition.
Example style:
“त्यो रात गाउँको पुरानो इनारबाट आएको आवाजले सबैलाई डरायो… तर त्यो आवाज सबैभन्दा पहिले सुन्ने व्यक्ति थियो दस वर्षको रोहन।”

Do not use foreign cities, foreign villages or generic Western settings.
3. Natural Nepali Dialogues
All dialogues must sound like real Nepali people speaking.
Use simple, emotional, natural Nepali language.
Example:
माया: “आमा, तपाईंले मसँग यति ठूलो कुरा किन लुकाउनुभयो?”
आमा: “छोरी… कहिलेकाहीँ सत्य लुकाउनु पनि कसैलाई बचाउनुजस्तै हुन्छ।”
Avoid overly formal, unnatural or textbook-style Nepali.
4. Every Scene Must End With Suspense
Every scene must end with one of the following:
A question
A mystery
A shocking discovery
An emotional trigger
A hidden secret
A betrayal
A dangerous decision
A surprising revelation
The audience must constantly think:
“अब के हुन्छ?”
5. Strong Emotional Connection
Make the audience deeply care about the main character.
Include moments of:
Happiness
Fear
Anger
Sadness
Betrayal
Hope
Sacrifice
Forgiveness
Surprise
6. At Least 3 Major Twists
Include at least three major twists that are surprising but logical.
Possible examples:
A trusted friend secretly betrayed the hero.
A poor-looking stranger knows the family’s hidden secret.
The person believed to be evil was actually protecting someone.
A missing animal or object reveals the truth.
The main character discovers a hidden family secret.
The apparent villain sacrifices something important in the end.

8. Detailed Story Structure
### Scene 1: Viral Hook
Immediately create curiosity.
### Scene 2: Mystery Begins
Introduce the strange problem, secret or danger.
### Scene 3: Emotional Conflict
Show the main character’s personal struggle.
### Scene 4: First Betrayal
Someone trusted causes pain or creates a serious problem.
### Scene 5: First Major Twist
Reveal something unexpected.
### Scene 6: Struggle
The hero must fight through fear, poverty, loneliness, danger or betrayal.
### Scene 7: Second Major Twist
Reveal a hidden truth.
### Scene 8: Biggest Betrayal or Emotional Breakdown
The main character reaches their lowest point.
### Scene 9: Third Major Twist
Reveal the truth behind the entire mystery.
### Scene 10: Unexpected Climax
Create a powerful emotional confrontation or surprising solution.
### Scene 11: Emotional Resolution
Give the audience justice, forgiveness, happiness or emotional satisfaction.
### Scene 12: Powerful Moral
End with a memorable moral lesson in simple Nepali.
### Writing Style:
Use cinematic narration.
Use natural Nepali dialogues.
Keep paragraphs short and voiceover-friendly.
Make every scene visually clear for 2D animation.
Add sound and atmosphere suggestions where useful:
[हावा चलिरहेको आवाज]

[गाउँको वातावरण]
[सस्पेन्स म्युजिक]
Do not write boring exposition.
Keep the pacing fast and engaging.
Make the story suitable for children and families.
Avoid graphic violence, adult content and inappropriate themes.
Make emotional moments powerful but family-friendly.
The ending must feel rewarding, emotional and memorable.
